Our History

100ClubSA was founded in 2015 with a clear mission: to empower the local community of Mankweng through accessible, professional, and inclusive fitness services. Recognizing a growing demand for health and wellness resources in the area, a team of passionate fitness enthusiasts and entrepreneurs came together to create a space that supports individuals in their journey toward physical and mental well-being. In its early days, the gym started with basic workout equipment and a small client base of dedicated locals. Despite limited resources, 100ClubSA quickly gained a reputation for its friendly environment, clean facilities, and supportive staff. It became especially popular among university students, professionals, and older adults looking to improve their health in a safe and motivating space. Over the years, the gym has steadily grown, expanding its range of services to include personal training, group fitness classes, and nutritional guidance. By 2022, 100ClubSA had introduced new strength and cardio machines, enhancing the member experience and allowing the gym to accommodate more visitors during peak hours. One of the gym's core values has always been community empowerment. It regularly hosts health awareness campaigns, fitness challenges, and school outreach programs to promote healthy living beyond its walls. It also prides itself on being inclusive and accessible, welcoming people of all ages, body types, and fitness levels. Today, 100ClubSA stands as a trusted fitness centre in Limpopo, committed to evolving with the needs of its members while staying true to its founding vision: to be more than just a gym, but a lifestyle partner for everyone striving to be healthier, stronger, and more confident.
